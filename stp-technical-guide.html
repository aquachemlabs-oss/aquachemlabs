STP Plant Engineering Guide

Engineering reference for sewage treatment plant design, biological process control, clarification, disinfection and sludge handling.

Open detailed PDF guide Ask an engineer

Sewage characterization

Peak flow, BOD, COD, TSS, ammonia and variability determine hydraulic and biological design.

Biological treatment

Maintain the required oxygen transfer, biomass concentration and sludge age for the selected process.

Clarification and disinfection

Secondary clarification must protect downstream disinfection; UV or chemical disinfection is selected to meet reuse or discharge requirements.

Operations

Routine checks of DO, pH, MLSS, settling and sludge wasting provide early warning of process instability.
