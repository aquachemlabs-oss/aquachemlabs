Pretreatment

Feed quality and pretreatment protect ion-exchange and EDI modules from hardness, organics and particulates.

DI systems

Mixed-bed or separate-bed ion exchange produces high-purity water when regeneration and rinsing are controlled.

EDI systems

Electrodeionization continuously removes ions using electricity, resin and selective membranes without chemical regeneration in the EDI module.
