2. Effluent Treatment Plant (ETP)

ETPs treat industrial wastewater before reuse, discharge or further polishing. Performance depends on influent characteristics, hydraulic loading, biological/chemical treatment, solids separation and disciplined operation.

Common problems / symptoms

  • High COD/BOD, suspended solids, oil & grease, colour or variable pH in influent.
  • Poor coagulation/flocculation causing cloudy outlet and weak sludge settling.
  • Foaming, odour, biological upset or inconsistent dissolved-oxygen conditions.
  • Excess sludge, poor dewatering or sludge carryover.
  • High outlet TSS/COD and frequent process instability.

Solutions we provide

  • Water and wastewater characterization to identify the actual pollutant load and treatment requirement.
  • Coagulant/flocculant selection and jar-test based dosing optimization.
  • pH correction and chemical conditioning where required.
  • Defoamer, biocide or odour-control support for specific operating conditions.
  • Biological-process support through appropriate nutrients/cultures where applicable.
  • Troubleshooting of aeration, clarification, filtration and sludge-handling stages.
  • Preventive monitoring plan for pH, ORP/DO where applicable, MLSS, settling, TSS, COD/BOD and sludge characteristics.

Recommended monitoring & checks

  • Influent/outlet pH and flow
  • COD/BOD and TSS
  • MLSS/settling behaviour for biological stages
  • DO/ORP where applicable
  • Sludge production and dewatering performance
  • Chemical consumption per m³

Relevant chemical / consumable support

  • Coagulants and flocculants
  • pH adjusters
  • Defoamers
  • Biocides
  • Nutrients / bacterial cultures
  • Filter aids and application-specific process chemicals

3. Sewage Treatment Plant (STP)

STPs treat domestic sewage using combinations of screening, biological treatment, clarification, filtration and disinfection. Stable biology and good solids separation are central to consistent performance.

Common problems / symptoms

  • Bad odour, septic conditions or floating solids.
  • High BOD/COD or TSS in treated water.
  • Poor settling, sludge bulking or sludge carryover.
  • Low dissolved oxygen or biological imbalance.
  • Frequent filter choking and inconsistent disinfection.

Solutions we provide

  • Review of hydraulic loading, aeration and biological operating conditions.
  • Biological culture/nutrient support when justified by process conditions.
  • Coagulation/flocculation assistance for polishing or difficult suspended solids.
  • pH correction and odour-control strategies.
  • Filtration and disinfection support, including cleaning and preventive maintenance.
  • Operator guidance for sludge wasting, aeration and routine testing.

Recommended monitoring & checks

  • Flow, pH and DO
  • MLSS/settling
  • BOD/COD and TSS
  • Sludge age/wasting practice
  • Filter differential pressure
  • Disinfection residual where applicable

Relevant chemical / consumable support

  • Bacterial culture
  • Nutrients
  • Coagulants/flocculants
  • Defoamers
  • Odour-control chemicals
  • Cleaning and disinfection chemicals

4. Reverse Osmosis (RO) Plant

RO systems remove dissolved salts and many contaminants through semi-permeable membranes. Stable pretreatment, correct recovery, pressure control and membrane cleaning are essential to protect flux and product-water quality.

Common problems / symptoms

  • Rising differential pressure or feed pressure.
  • Falling permeate flow or increasing normalized pressure drop.
  • Increasing permeate conductivity / salt passage.
  • Membrane scaling from hardness, silica or other sparingly soluble salts.
  • Organic, colloidal or biological fouling.
  • Frequent cartridge-filter choking.

Solutions we provide

  • Raw-water analysis and RO operating review.
  • Antiscalant selection and dose optimization based on feed chemistry and recovery.
  • Dechlorination where chlorine-sensitive membranes are used.
  • Membrane cleaning programme using compatible acidic/alkaline or specialty cleaners as required.
  • pH adjustment and pretreatment optimization.
  • Monitoring of feed/permeate conductivity, flow, pressure and recovery to identify deterioration early.

Recommended monitoring & checks

  • Feed/permeate conductivity
  • Normalized permeate flow
  • Pressure and differential pressure
  • Recovery
  • pH and temperature
  • SDI/turbidity after pretreatment

Relevant chemical / consumable support

  • RO antiscalants
  • RO membrane cleaners
  • pH adjusters
  • Biocides where appropriate
  • Dechlorination chemicals
  • Cartridge/spun/wound filter consumables

5. Boiler Water Treatment

Boilers concentrate dissolved and suspended impurities. Without a controlled water-treatment programme, scale, corrosion, carryover and poor heat transfer can reduce efficiency and damage equipment.

Common problems / symptoms

  • Hard-scale deposits on heat-transfer surfaces.
  • Corrosion caused by dissolved oxygen, low/high pH or unsuitable chemistry.
  • Foaming and carryover into steam systems.
  • High TDS/conductivity and inadequate blowdown control.
  • Frequent tube cleaning and declining heat-transfer efficiency.

Solutions we provide

  • Feedwater and boiler-water analysis with a site-specific treatment programme.
  • Scale-control and dispersant chemistry to reduce deposit formation.
  • Oxygen-scavenger programme where appropriate.
  • pH/alkalinity control and blowdown optimization.
  • Boiler descaling and cleaning support during shutdown maintenance.
  • Monitoring plan covering conductivity/TDS, pH, phosphate or other programme-specific parameters.

Recommended monitoring & checks

  • Feedwater hardness and alkalinity
  • Boiler-water conductivity/TDS
  • pH
  • Dissolved oxygen where relevant
  • Blowdown rate
  • Steam quality/carryover indicators

Relevant chemical / consumable support

  • Oxygen scavengers
  • pH boosters/alkalinity control chemicals
  • Scale inhibitors/dispersants
  • Corrosion inhibitors
  • Boiler descalants
  • Condensate-treatment chemicals where required

6. Cooling Tower Water Treatment

Cooling towers recirculate water while rejecting heat. Evaporation concentrates dissolved solids, while warm wet surfaces can support microbiological growth. A balanced programme controls scale, corrosion and biological fouling.

Common problems / symptoms

  • Scale deposits on fill, exchangers, nozzles or piping.
  • Corrosion of metal components.
  • Slime, algae, biofilm or unpleasant odour.
  • High conductivity/TDS from concentration cycles.
  • Poor heat transfer and increased approach temperature.

Solutions we provide

  • Cooling-water analysis and cycle-of-concentration review.
  • Scale inhibitor/dispersant selection for the actual water chemistry.
  • Corrosion inhibitor programme compatible with metallurgy and operating conditions.
  • Biocide strategy with appropriate dosing and monitoring.
  • pH and conductivity control, including blowdown optimization.
  • Cleaning support and preventive inspection of basin, fill, nozzles and strainers.

Recommended monitoring & checks

  • pH and conductivity
  • Cycles of concentration
  • Make-up/blowdown flow
  • Microbiological indicators
  • Corrosion/scale observations
  • Cooling performance / approach

Relevant chemical / consumable support

  • Scale inhibitors
  • Corrosion inhibitors
  • Biocides
  • Dispersants
  • pH controllers/adjusters
  • Cleaning chemicals

7. Water Softener

Water softeners exchange hardness ions such as calcium and magnesium for sodium ions. Correct regeneration and resin care are necessary to maintain soft-water quality and protect downstream equipment.

Common problems / symptoms

  • Hardness leakage in outlet water.
  • Premature resin exhaustion.
  • Inadequate brine strength or poor regeneration.
  • Channeling, fouling or pressure drop across the vessel.
  • High hardness entering RO or boiler pretreatment.

Solutions we provide

  • Hardness testing and regeneration-cycle optimization.
  • Resin selection/replacement based on feed chemistry and service conditions.
  • Brine preparation and regeneration support.
  • Resin cleaning where fouling is identified.
  • Valve, distributor and flow-control inspection.
  • Preventive monitoring of inlet/outlet hardness and pressure drop.

Recommended monitoring & checks

  • Inlet/outlet hardness
  • Flow rate
  • Regeneration frequency
  • Brine concentration/consumption
  • Pressure drop
  • Resin condition

Relevant chemical / consumable support

  • Salt / regeneration chemicals
  • Resin cleaners
  • Anti-fouling support chemicals
  • Application-specific cleaning chemicals

8. Demineralization (DM) System

Demineralization removes ionic impurities using cation and anion exchange, commonly followed by mixed-bed polishing where very high-purity water is required. Regeneration chemistry and resin condition strongly affect outlet quality.

Common problems / symptoms

  • Rising outlet conductivity or silica leakage.
  • Shorter run length between regenerations.
  • Resin fouling, oxidation or physical degradation.
  • Incorrect regeneration sequence or chemical concentration.
  • Poor separation/mixing in mixed-bed systems.

Solutions we provide

  • Water analysis and resin-capacity review.
  • Cation/anion resin selection, servicing and replacement support.
  • Regeneration-programme optimization and chemical handling guidance.
  • Resin cleaning for identified organic, iron or other fouling conditions.
  • Mixed-bed polishing support where required.
  • Routine monitoring of conductivity, silica and regeneration performance.

Recommended monitoring & checks

  • Conductivity/resistivity
  • Silica where relevant
  • pH
  • Regeneration chemical consumption
  • Run length
  • Resin condition

Relevant chemical / consumable support

  • Cation and anion exchange resins
  • Regenerant chemicals
  • pH adjustment chemicals
  • Resin cleaning chemicals
  • Application-specific polishing consumables

10. Integrated Troubleshooting Approach

When a treatment plant underperforms, changing chemicals alone may not solve the problem. We use a structured approach that connects water quality, process conditions, equipment condition and chemical dosing.

  1. 1. Identify the symptomDefine the actual deviation: high conductivity, hardness leakage, high COD/TSS, scale, corrosion, odour, pressure drop, poor settling, etc.
  2. 2. Test the waterCompare current water chemistry with design/normal operating data.
  3. 3. Check the processReview flow, loading, pressure, temperature, recovery, aeration, blowdown and regeneration cycles as applicable.
  4. 4. Inspect equipmentCheck pumps, dosing systems, valves, filters, membranes, resin vessels, heat-transfer surfaces and instrumentation.
  5. 5. Optimize the treatmentSelect compatible chemicals and dosing based on the actual problem and process constraints.
  6. 6. Verify performanceTrend key parameters and confirm that the intervention produces a stable improvement.

Typical service outcomes to target

  • More consistent treated-water quality.
  • Control of scale, corrosion and microbiological fouling.
  • Better membrane, boiler, heat-exchanger and piping protection.
  • Reduced unplanned cleaning and maintenance caused by preventable water-quality issues.
  • Improved chemical-dosing discipline and operating visibility.
  • Support for environmental and process-quality objectives.
