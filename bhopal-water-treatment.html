Planning a water-treatment project in Bhopal

Before selecting equipment, identify the water source, actual chemistry, required treated-water quality and the process that consumes the water. Industrial requirements can include RO for dissolved-solids reduction, DM/DI polishing for low-conductivity water, filtration for suspended solids, boiler and cooling-tower chemistry, or ETP/STP treatment for wastewater and reuse.

Engineering checklist

  • Provide a current laboratory report and note seasonal changes or multiple water sources.
  • State average and peak flow, storage, operating hours and expected future capacity.
  • For boilers, provide feed-water and condensate data, pressure and current treatment practices.
  • For cooling towers, provide make-up chemistry, circulating conductivity, cycles, metallurgy and visible scale/corrosion symptoms.
  • For wastewater, provide process description, representative flow and analytical results with the applicable discharge/reuse criteria.

Site-specific assessment determines whether the project needs new equipment, pretreatment changes, instrumentation, chemical optimization, commissioning or ongoing maintenance. Recovery and outlet quality should be agreed against a documented design basis and verified by testing.
