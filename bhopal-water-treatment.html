Service programmes

Do you design from water analysis?

Yes. A recent feed-water or effluent analysis is the preferred starting point for sizing and chemical-programme decisions, alongside flow, operating conditions and required treated-water quality.

Can you support an existing plant?

Yes. Share plant photos, current operating data, water analysis and the specific problem so the team can distinguish process, equipment, chemistry and dosing issues.

Can I request TDS, SDS or COA documents?

Yes. Applicable documents can be requested for the exact product, concentration or batch. See the technical document centre.
