Frequently asked questions

Do you design industrial water treatment plants?

Yes. Plant selection and engineering are based on source-water or wastewater analysis, flow, required outlet quality and operating conditions.

Can you commission and maintain existing plants?

Yes. Commissioning, troubleshooting, chemical optimisation and maintenance support can be scoped for existing installations.

Can I request a technical guide?

Yes. Each major service has an online engineering summary and a detailed PDF guide.

How do I request a proposal?

Use the enquiry form and include flow, water analysis and the treatment objective where available.
