Our Services

Descaling, erection, commissioning, maintenance and water testing.

Water treatment, from design to maintenance

We design, supply, install, commission and operate complete industrial treatment plants across Madhya Pradesh and central India.

Effluent Treatment Plants (ETP)
Wastewater characterization, coagulant optimization, aeration & biological load management.
Sewage Treatment Plants (STP)
Screening, biological balance, clarification, disinfection, and sludge handling systems.
Reverse Osmosis (RO) Plants
Dissolved salt removal, antiscalants, membrane fouling control, CIP cleaning and maintenance.
Boiler Water Treatment
Scale & corrosion inhibitors, oxygen scavengers, alkalinity boosters and blowdown optimization.
Cooling Tower Water Treatment
Cycles of concentration review, biocide management, corrosion and scale control programs.
Water Softener & DM Plants
Ion-exchange resin regeneration, iron fouling cleaning, demineralisation and mixed bed units.
Zero Liquid Discharge (ZLD)
Advanced recovery systems to recycle process water and eliminate liquid discharge.
Filtration & Clarification
Sand, carbon, cartridge, bag, ultrafiltration, and multi-grade suspended solids removal.
