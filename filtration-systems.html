Design & operating considerations

Loading rate

Hydraulic and solids loading determine media area and backwash frequency.

Media selection

Media depth and type should be matched to particle loading and target water quality.

Backwash

Backwash flow and sequence must be sufficient to expand or clean the bed without media loss.

Downstream protection

Fine filtration is particularly important ahead of pumps, membranes, dosing equipment and sensitive process users.

Frequently asked questions

Should activated carbon be used on every system?

No. Carbon is useful for certain organic compounds and chlorine removal, but it also adds biological and pressure-drop considerations.

Why does a filter pressure drop increase?

Common causes are accumulated solids, fouling, media compaction, insufficient backwash or inappropriate filtration loading.

Can filtration remove dissolved salts?

Conventional media filters target suspended particles and certain contaminants; dissolved salt reduction generally requires ion exchange, membrane or other specialised treatment.
