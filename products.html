How our chemicals work

Water treatment chemicals and their functions

Every chemical programme is selected for the plant it protects. See what each of our boiler, cooling tower, ETP / STP and RO chemicals does.

ACL BOILER CHEMICALSACL COOLING TOWER CHEMICALSACL ETP / STP WASTEWATER CHEMICALSRO Water Treatment Chemicals

ACL BOILER CHEMICALS

Protect boiler tubes, drums and condensate lines from scale, corrosion and carry-over while improving fuel efficiency.

01

ACL PH BOOSTER

Raises and holds boiler water alkalinity in the correct range so steel surfaces stay passive and acidic corrosion is prevented.

02

ACL SLUDGE CONDITIONER

Keeps hardness salts and iron as soft, non-sticky sludge that stays suspended and leaves with blowdown instead of baking onto tubes.

03

ACL ANTI-SCALANTS

Sequester calcium, magnesium and silica to stop hard scale forming on heating surfaces, keeping heat transfer high and fuel use low.

04

ACL OXYGEN SCAVENGERS

Remove dissolved oxygen from feed water to prevent pitting corrosion in the boiler, economiser and feed lines.

05

ACL FIRESIDE CHEMICALS

Condition soot, slag and clinker on the fire side, improving combustion and keeping flue passages and tubes clean.

06

ACL ANTIFOAMS

Break foam in boiler water to prevent priming and carry-over of water and dissolved solids into the steam.

07

ACL CORROSION INHIBITORS

Form a protective film on boiler and condensate-line metal to prevent corrosion caused by oxygen and carbon dioxide.

08

ACL DESCALING CHEMICALS

Dissolve existing scale and rust deposits during shutdown cleaning, restoring heat transfer and extending tube life.

09

ACL METAL PASSIVATOR / NEUTRALIZERS

Neutralise residual acid after descaling and rebuild a passive oxide layer so freshly cleaned metal does not flash-rust.

ACL COOLING TOWER CHEMICALS

Keep heat exchangers, condensers and fills clean and corrosion-free so the cooling circuit delivers design approach temperature.

01

ACL CORROSION INHIBITORS

Phosphate, zinc and alloy-based programmes form a protective film on mild steel, copper and alloy surfaces of exchangers and pipelines.

02

ACL SCALE INHIBITOR

Keeps calcium carbonate, sulphate and silica in solution at higher cycles of concentration, preventing scale and saving make-up water.

03

Biocides (oxidizing & non-oxidizing)

Kill bacteria, fungi and Legionella in recirculating water; alternating both types prevents microbial resistance.

04

ACL ALGAECIDES

Control algae on basins, decks, distribution channels and fills exposed to sunlight, preventing blockage and fouling.

05

Bio Dispersants (foaming & non-foaming)

Loosen and lift biofilm and slime so biocides penetrate better and debris is flushed out with blowdown.

06

ACL OIL DISPERSANTS

Emulsify oil and grease leaking in from the process so they do not coat heat-exchanger surfaces or foul fill media.

07

ACL BIO DETERGENTS

Clean organic and biological fouling from system surfaces during online or offline cleaning to restore heat-transfer efficiency.

ACL ETP / STP WASTEWATER CHEMICALS

Help effluent and sewage treatment plants meet discharge norms for COD, BOD, TSS, colour and odour, and handle sludge efficiently.

01

ACL POLY ELECTROLYTES

Cationic and anionic polymers bridge fine particles into large, fast-settling flocs in clarifiers, improving TSS and COD removal.

02

ACL SLUDGE DEWATERING

Conditions sludge before the filter press, belt press or centrifuge so water separates easily and a drier cake is produced.

03

ACL WATER DECOLORING

Removes dye and colour from textile, paper, pharma and distillery effluent by coagulating colour molecules for separation.

04

ACL DEFOAMERS

Knock down foam in aeration, equalisation and outlet tanks, preventing overflow and improving oxygen transfer.

05

ACL LIME / ALUM

Lime neutralises acidic effluent and precipitates heavy metals; alum coagulates suspended solids and phosphate in primary treatment.

06

ACL BIO CULTURES

Specialised bacteria and nutrients seed and boost biological treatment, speeding start-up, improving BOD/COD reduction and controlling odour.

RO Water Treatment Chemicals

Protect RO and UF membranes from scaling, fouling and chlorine damage so the plant keeps its output, recovery and salt rejection.

01

ACL RO ANTISCALANTS

High/low silica and high/low pH grades prevent carbonate, sulphate, silica and metal scale on membranes, allowing higher recovery.

02

ACL LOW PH CLEANERS

Acidic cleaners dissolve inorganic scale and metal oxides such as calcium carbonate and iron from the membrane surface.

03

ACL HIGH PH CLEANERS

Alkaline cleaners remove organic fouling, biofilm and colloidal silt that reduce permeate flow.

04

ACL C.I.P CHEMICALS

Complete clean-in-place programmes restore flux and salt rejection without dismantling membranes from the pressure vessels.

05

ACL CHLORINE INHIBITORS

Neutralise residual chlorine in RO feed water to protect polyamide membranes from irreversible oxidation damage.

06

ACL CHLORINATING AGENTS

Disinfect raw water and pre-treatment to control bacterial growth before filtration and membranes.

07

ACL ULTRA FILTRATION CLEANING CHEMICALS

Clean UF membranes of organics, biofouling and particulates to recover permeability and backwash efficiency.

08

ACL HYDROCHLORIC ACID (HCl)

Lowers feed pH to limit carbonate scaling and is used for acid cleaning and cation resin regeneration.

09

ACL CAUSTIC (NaOH)

Raises pH for alkaline membrane cleaning and silica control, and regenerates anion exchange resin.

10

ACL HYPOCHLORITE

Oxidising disinfectant for raw water, storage tanks and pre-treatment sanitisation.

11

ACL SODIUM META BI-SULPHITE

Dechlorinates RO feed water and preserves membranes during storage or plant shutdown.
