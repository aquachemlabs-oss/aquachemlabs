RO chemical programme: selection and control

Reverse-osmosis chemical programmes commonly include antiscalant, membrane cleaning chemicals and upstream dechlorination where required. The programme depends on feed-water analysis, membrane material, recovery, temperature, pretreatment and the scaling/fouling risks identified by a membrane projection. No single antiscalant or dose is suitable for every water source.

Data needed before selection

  • Complete feed analysis including hardness, alkalinity, silica, sulphate, barium/strontium where relevant, iron, manganese, pH and temperature.
  • Membrane model, array configuration, feed flow, recovery, concentrate flow and operating pressure.
  • Existing dosing point, pump calibration, dilution water quality and actual product consumption.
  • Normalized permeate flow, salt passage and stage differential pressure trends.

Safe operating practice

Verify chemical compatibility with membranes and system materials. Calibrate dosing pumps, confirm injection-point condition and follow the product label and safety data sheet. Cleaning should be based on performance symptoms and the membrane manufacturer's limits, with the correct cleaner chosen for the suspected foulant. Avoid increasing dose or changing chemicals without checking the water chemistry and operating data.
