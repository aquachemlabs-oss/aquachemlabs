Industrial water-treatment buyers need more than a product list. This page organizes project evidence around engineering scope, water chemistry, operating targets and measurable outcomes.

ETP

Effluent treatment

Engineering scope: equalization, physicochemical treatment, biological treatment, clarification and sludge management.

Evidence to capture: flow/KLD, influent COD/BOD/TSS, treatment train, chemical programme, outlet quality and operating cost.

STP

Sewage treatment

Engineering scope: screening, biological treatment, clarification, disinfection and reuse/discharge support.

Evidence to capture: design flow, influent characteristics, biological process, treated-water quality and reuse objective.

RO

Reverse osmosis

Engineering scope: pretreatment, cartridge filtration, high-pressure RO, recovery control, CIP and water-quality monitoring.

Evidence to capture: feed analysis, capacity/LPH, recovery, permeate conductivity/TDS, pressure profile and membrane performance.

Boiler water treatment

Engineering scope: feedwater conditioning, chemical dosing, blowdown optimization and scale/corrosion monitoring.

Evidence to capture: boiler pressure, feedwater chemistry, cycles/blowdown, chemical programme and deposit/corrosion observations.

Cooling-water treatment

Engineering scope: scale/corrosion control, microbiological control and cycles-of-concentration management.

Evidence to capture: makeup-water chemistry, cycles, conductivity, microbiological monitoring, chemical programme and metallurgy.

Filtration & clarification

How to build a strong case study

Document every project with measurable evidence

For completed installations, the strongest case studies should show the starting water chemistry, design capacity, treatment sequence, chemical programme, commissioning observations and before/after operating results. Client identity can be anonymized when confidentiality requires it.
