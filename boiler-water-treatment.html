Engineering approach

Protect heat transfer and steam quality

Boiler problems rarely come from one parameter. Hardness leakage, dissolved oxygen, alkalinity, silica, phosphate or other conditioning chemistry, concentration cycles and blowdown all interact. Descaling should follow a controlled assessment of deposit type and equipment material.

Design & operating considerations

Pretreatment

Reducing hardness and other contaminants upstream lowers the scaling burden on the boiler.

Chemical dosing

Product selection and dose targets should be based on boiler type, pressure, feedwater and operating programme.

Blowdown

Conductivity and chemistry trends help control dissolved solids and concentration.

Descaling

Choose cleaning chemistry from deposit composition and metallurgy; isolate, flush and neutralise according to the equipment manufacturer's requirements.

Frequently asked questions

What causes boiler scale?

Hardness salts and other deposits can precipitate on hot surfaces when feedwater contaminants concentrate or treatment is inadequate.

How is boiler blowdown controlled?

Blowdown is typically managed from water-quality indicators such as conductivity and chemistry limits established for the boiler operating regime.

Can you descaling an existing boiler?

Descaling can be planned after identifying deposit type, boiler metallurgy, operating history and safe cleaning/neutralisation requirements.
