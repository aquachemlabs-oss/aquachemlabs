ETP Plant Engineering Guide

Engineering reference for industrial ETP design, wastewater characterization, biological treatment, clarification and troubleshooting.

Open detailed PDF guide Ask an engineer

Wastewater characterization

Design begins with flow, COD, BOD, TSS, pH, oil, colour, temperature and site-specific contaminants. Sampling quality matters as much as the laboratory result.

Process selection

Equalization, coagulation, biological treatment, clarification, filtration and sludge handling are selected around the wastewater load and required treated-water quality.

Chemical control

Jar testing and operating data should determine coagulant and polymer selection; chemical dosing is not a substitute for correct hydraulics and biological balance.

Troubleshooting

Track DO, MLSS, SVI, sludge blanket, pH and load changes before changing multiple process variables at once.

ETP design inputs and process checks

Effluent treatment design begins with a representative wastewater profile and an understanding of production schedules. Record average and peak flow, pH, temperature, COD, BOD, TSS, oil and grease, colour, nutrients, dissolved salts and any process-specific contaminants. A single grab sample may not represent batch discharges or production changeovers; sampling plans should capture normal and peak conditions.

Typical treatment stages

  • Screening and equalization to manage solids and flow variation.
  • pH correction and, where required, coagulation/flocculation followed by clarification.
  • Biological treatment where biodegradable organic load requires it, with aeration and biomass control matched to the selected process.
  • Secondary clarification, filtration or polishing and disinfection where the reuse or discharge specification requires them.
  • Sludge thickening, dewatering and compliant handling of the resulting solids.

Operational monitoring

Trend inlet and outlet flow, pH, COD/BOD results, dissolved oxygen where biological treatment is used, sludge settling, clarifier carryover, filter differential pressure and chemical consumption. Jar tests help compare coagulants and polymer grades, but dosage should be confirmed on actual wastewater and rechecked when the production mix changes. Avoid selecting chemicals from colour or appearance alone.

Final process selection must satisfy the applicable consent conditions and discharge/reuse limits. See ETP plant services, ETP/STP chemicals, STP engineering and request a technical review.
