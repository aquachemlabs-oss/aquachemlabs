Design & operating considerations

Peak flow

Design should account for diurnal variation and hydraulic peaks, not only average daily flow.

Aeration

Air delivery and DO control are central to stable biological oxidation.

Sludge

Return and wasting strategies affect MLSS, settling and treatment stability.

Reuse

Filtration and disinfection should be matched to the intended reuse application.

Frequently asked questions

What causes odour in an STP?

Common causes include septic conditions, inadequate aeration, overloaded or poorly mixed zones, excessive solids accumulation and poor sludge handling.

Can treated STP water be reused?

Reuse depends on final water quality, the receiving application and applicable local requirements. Tertiary filtration and disinfection are often used for higher-quality reuse.

How should an STP be maintained?

Routine checks should cover flow, pH, DO, MLSS, sludge wasting, blower operation, settling behaviour, filtration and disinfection performance.
