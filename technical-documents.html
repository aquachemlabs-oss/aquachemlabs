Document-control rule

TDS, SDS and COA documents are product-, concentration-, batch- or application-specific. They should not be copied between chemicals or treated as universal operating instructions. Final dosing, discharge limits and plant settings must be confirmed against current water analysis, equipment/OEM limits and applicable safety requirements.
