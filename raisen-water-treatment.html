Water-treatment engineering support in Raisen

For an industrial or institutional water-treatment project, begin with a current raw-water report, required treated-water quality and the flow demand. The correct solution may involve filtration, softening, RO, DM/DI polishing, boiler or cooling-tower treatment, or wastewater treatment and reuse. Equipment and chemical programmes should be selected to match the actual chemistry and operating conditions.

Project information that helps

  • Source water and laboratory analysis, with sampling date and units.
  • Average and peak capacity, operating hours, storage and planned expansion.
  • Target water quality and where the treated water will be used.
  • Existing plant drawings, equipment nameplates, alarm history and maintenance records.
  • For wastewater, flow variation, process description, analytical results and applicable discharge/reuse criteria.

Support may include site assessment, design review, equipment supply, commissioning, operator training, chemical optimization and annual maintenance planning. Scope and performance commitments are confirmed against a documented design basis and site conditions.
