Industrial treatment systems for Raisen

Solutions can include industrial RO plants, water-treatment and filtration systems, ETP and STP plants, DM and softener systems, boiler-water treatment and cooling-tower chemical programmes. Final equipment selection and chemical dosing should be based on current water analysis, required flow, target water quality and site conditions.

Water Treatment Chemicals

Application-focused programmes for RO, boiler, cooling tower and wastewater treatment.

Technical support

For an existing plant, share recent water analysis, plant capacity, operating conditions, photographs and the problem being investigated. The technical team can then assess process, equipment and chemistry requirements.
