Plant manufacturing

What each plant does

Hover over a card or select it to reveal its operating function.

ETP

Effluent Treatment Plant

View function

Treats industrial wastewater by removing suspended solids, oils, chemicals, COD/BOD and other process contaminants before discharge or reuse.

STP

Sewage Treatment Plant

View function

Uses biological and physical treatment to reduce organic matter, solids and pathogens in domestic sewage for safe disposal or reuse.

RO

Reverse Osmosis Plant

View function

Forces water through selective membranes to reduce dissolved salts, hardness and contaminants for process-quality water.

DM / DI

DM & Deionization Plant

View function

Uses cation and anion exchange to remove dissolved minerals, producing low-conductivity water for boilers and precision processes.

Softener

Water Softening Plant

View function

Exchanges calcium and magnesium hardness for sodium to prevent scale in pipelines, heat exchangers and production equipment.

UF

Ultrafiltration System

View function

Membrane filtration removes fine suspended solids, colloids and microorganisms, often protecting downstream RO systems.

MMF / PSF

Multimedia & Pressure Sand Filters

View function

Layered sand, gravel and anthracite beds trap turbidity and suspended solids as the first stage of pre-treatment.

ACF

Activated Carbon Filter

View function

Adsorbs free chlorine, organics, colour and odour to protect membranes and resins and improve treated-water quality.

Micron

Micron & Cartridge Filtration

View function

Fine cartridge elements capture residual particles before membranes, dosing points and critical process equipment.

Boiler

Boiler Feed Water Treatment

View function

Conditions feed water to control hardness, silica, oxygen and pH, protecting boilers against scale, carry-over and corrosion.

Cooling

Cooling Tower Water Treatment

View function

Controls scale, corrosion, fouling and microbiological growth so heat-transfer surfaces remain efficient and reliable.

Process

Process Water Treatment Plant

View function

Delivers water of a defined quality for manufacturing, washing, formulation and utility applications.

Recycle

Wastewater Recycling System

View function

Upgrades treated effluent or sewage for reuse in cooling, gardening, flushing or process duties, reducing fresh-water demand.

ZLD

Zero Liquid Discharge Plant

View function

Combines membrane recovery, concentration and evaporation to reuse water and eliminate liquid effluent leaving the facility.

Custom

Customized Filtration & Recovery Systems

View function

Bespoke combinations of filtration, membrane and recovery technology engineered around a specific process challenge.

Every system can be engineered according to feed-water characteristics, process requirements, desired water quality, recovery targets, operating conditions and applicable regulatory requirements.

Water treatment chemicals

Application-led chemical programs

Hover or select a chemical family to understand its role.

01

RO antiscalants

Keep sparingly soluble salts dispersed so deposits do not block membrane surfaces or reduce recovery.

02

RO membrane cleaning chemicals

Acidic and alkaline programs remove mineral, organic and biological fouling during controlled CIP cleaning.

03

Coagulants & flocculants

Destabilize fine particles and bind them into larger flocs for effective clarification, settling and dewatering.

04

Boiler water treatment chemicals

Condition boiler water to control scale, sludge, alkalinity and carry-over for safe, efficient steam generation.

05

Cooling tower chemicals

Combined programs that keep recirculating water free of scale, corrosion, fouling and microbiological growth.

06

Corrosion inhibitors

Form protective films or control water chemistry to reduce metal loss in boilers and cooling circuits.

07

Scale inhibitors

Hold hardness salts in solution so they do not deposit on heat-transfer surfaces and pipelines.

08

Biocides & microbiological control

Control bacteria, algae and biofilm that cause fouling, odour, health risks and under-deposit corrosion.

09

Oxygen scavengers

Remove dissolved oxygen from boiler-feed water to protect metal surfaces against oxygen pitting.

10

pH control chemicals

Adjust acidity or alkalinity to optimize treatment reactions, protect assets and meet discharge limits.

11

ETP & STP treatment chemicals

Support biological and physico-chemical treatment with nutrients, cultures, decolourants and odour control.

12

Defoaming agents

Rapidly collapse unwanted process foam that interferes with aeration, separation and vessel capacity.

13

Membrane treatment chemicals

Dechlorination, preservation and sanitization products that extend membrane life between cleanings.

14

Process-specific specialty chemicals

Tailored formulations developed for a particular process, contaminant profile or operating constraint.

Programs are selected according to water chemistry, operating parameters, contaminant loading, temperature, pH, TDS, hardness, silica, alkalinity, microbiological activity and system-specific requirements.
