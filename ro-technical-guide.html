Industrial RO Plant Engineering Guide

Engineering reference for industrial RO pretreatment, membrane selection, recovery, cleaning, scaling and troubleshooting.

Feed-water analysis

TDS, hardness, alkalinity, silica, iron, manganese, SDI, temperature and microbiology affect pretreatment and membrane performance.

Pretreatment

Media filtration, cartridge filtration, antiscalant and dechlorination are selected to protect the membrane array.

Performance monitoring

Normalise permeate flow and salt passage for temperature and pressure so fouling is distinguished from seasonal changes.

CIP and recovery

Cleaning chemistry must match the fouling type and membrane manufacturer limits; recovery targets must respect scaling and operating constraints.

Design checks before selecting an industrial RO system

Start with a representative raw-water laboratory report and confirm whether the source changes seasonally. At minimum, review conductivity or TDS, pH, temperature, hardness, alkalinity, silica, sulphate, chloride, iron, manganese, turbidity and SDI where applicable. The membrane projection should use the actual feed chemistry and required permeate quality, not a generic recovery assumption.

Operating indicators to trend

  • Feed, concentrate and permeate flow; calculate recovery from measured flows and verify instrument units.
  • Feed pressure, stage pressure drop and differential pressure across cartridge filters.
  • Permeate conductivity and salt passage, interpreted with temperature and pressure normalization.
  • Antiscalant dosing, dechlorination performance and pretreatment filter/backwash history.

When to investigate performance loss

A sustained decline in normalized permeate flow, rising normalized salt passage or increasing pressure drop can indicate different failure modes. Compare trends against the commissioning baseline, check instrumentation and pretreatment first, and use the membrane manufacturer's cleaning criteria. Cleaning chemistry, pH, temperature and contact time must remain within the membrane supplier's limits. Do not increase recovery or dose chemicals blindly to compensate for a deteriorating plant.
