Industrial Water Treatment Plant (WTP)

Engineered raw-water treatment for clarification, filtration and conditioning before process use or downstream RO.

Typical treatment train

Raw-water analysis

Source-water testing defines turbidity, hardness, iron, manganese, alkalinity, TDS and microbiological requirements.

Clarification

Coagulation, flocculation and settling reduce suspended solids before filtration.

Media filtration

Pressure or gravity filters remove remaining particulate matter and improve downstream reliability.

Disinfection & polishing

Disinfection and application-specific polishing prepare water for storage, process use or RO pretreatment.

Plant sizing is based on source-water quality, required flow, peak demand, discharge requirements and downstream process sensitivity.
