How to select wastewater-treatment chemicals

Coagulants and polymers should be selected by wastewater characterization and jar testing, not by a generic dose. Record pH, temperature, suspended solids, colour, COD and the production conditions when sampling. The correct chemistry can change when raw material, cleaning cycles or production throughput changes.

Selection and verification

  • Compare candidate coagulants at controlled pH and mixing conditions.
  • Screen polymer charge type and dose using floc quality, settling, clarified-water quality and sludge dewatering response.
  • Confirm compatibility with biological treatment if chemicals are dosed upstream of a biological stage.
  • Track actual consumption per unit of treated flow and compare it with effluent quality.
  • Use safe chemical storage, dosing equipment, PPE and spill containment as required by the product safety data sheet.

Do not treat chemical addition as a substitute for correcting hydraulic overload, poor aeration, inadequate equalization or faulty clarification. Review the process with actual sample data and applicable discharge limits. See ETP plant services, STP plant services, plant chemical guide and request a jar-test review.
