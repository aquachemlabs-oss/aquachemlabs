Engineering & project execution

10-Stage Industrial Water Treatment Engineering Workflow

A structured engineering approach from source-water characterization through commissioning, performance testing, operations handover and sludge management.

  1. 01

    Source Characterization & Treatability Studies

    Assess feed-water or wastewater quality, variability, contaminants and treatment requirements before process selection.

  2. 02

    Define Regulatory & Output Targets

    Establish applicable discharge, reuse, process-water and operating targets for the project.

  3. 03

    Process Flow Selection (PFD)

    Select the treatment train and prepare the process flow diagram around the defined feed and output conditions.

  4. 04

    Mass Balance & Hydraulic Profiling

    Develop flow, load and water balances and verify hydraulic requirements across the treatment system.

  5. 05

    Detailed Multidisciplinary Engineering

    Coordinate process, mechanical, electrical, instrumentation, piping and civil engineering requirements.

  6. 06

    Plant Layout & 3D Modeling

    Plan equipment arrangement, access, maintenance clearances, piping routes and constructability.

  7. 07

    Civil Construction & Equipment Erection

    Execute civil works, equipment installation, piping, electrical systems and instrumentation with coordinated site activities.

  8. 08

    Pre-Commissioning (Dry & Wet Testing)

    Verify installation integrity, rotation, leak tightness, instrumentation, interlocks and wet-system readiness.

  9. 09

    Process Commissioning & PGTR

    Start the process, stabilize operating conditions and conduct performance guarantee testing and reporting where contracted.

  10. 10

    O&M Handover & Sludge Management

    Complete operator handover, documentation, maintenance guidance and practical sludge handling and disposal planning.
